How to play

  • Build with your Dozer / Worker — select it and pick a structure from the command grid (bottom right).
  • Money: build a Supply Center near supply piles; it produces Supply Trucks that haul crates automatically. Markets give a steady trickle.
  • Power: most structures need it — keep the ⚡ bar green or everything slows down and defenses go offline. The Scorpion Cartel needs no power at all.
  • Army: Barracks trains infantry, War Factory builds vehicles, Airfield builds strike jets (jets rearm at the airfield).
  • Promotions: combat earns ★ ranks — spend points on General's Powers (top bar): airstrikes, ambushes, carpet bombs…
  • Superweapon: each faction has one. Long countdown, devastating result. The enemy sees your timer — and you see theirs.
  • Domination mode: hold the central ⚑ point uncontested to earn ~30 pts/min — first team to 1,000 wins. The 💰 Trade Point gives your whole team +30% income. Annihilation also wins.
  • Tab toggles the live battle scoreboard (kills & army value for every general).
  • Win by reaching 1,000 domination points or destroying every enemy structure.
