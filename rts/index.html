HOW TO WIN

  • Build your economy. Supply trucks shuttle crates from supply docks to your Supply Center. Capture oil derricks with infantry for bonus income.
  • Watch your power (Coalition). Low power halves production speed and disables defenses. The Cartel needs no power.
  • Earn General's promotions. Combat experience unlocks powers — airstrikes, ambushes, artillery barrages.
  • Race the superweapons. Particle Cannon and Scud Storm timers are shown up top. Destroy them before they fire.
  • Destroy every enemy structure to win. Lose all of yours and the desert keeps your name.
